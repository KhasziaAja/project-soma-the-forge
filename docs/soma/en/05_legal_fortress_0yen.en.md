## 5.1 Dual-Mode Legal Protocol (JP & US Jurisdictions)

* **Japan Mode (JP Mode) Triple Separation Requirements:**
  1. **100% Legal & Capital Separation:** The "Housing Management Entity (Entity A)" granting gratuitous housing occupancy and the "Data & Guild Operating Entity (Entity B)" paying mining and guild rewards maintain zero overlapping equity, zero shared directors, and completely segregated accounting ledgers.
  2. **Civil Code Sec. 593 & Multi-Layer Statutory Defense:** The agreement between Entity A and the resident is executed strictly as a gratuitous "Loan for Use" (*Shiyou Taishaku*, Civil Code Sec. 593) devoid of monetary consideration. As a secondary fallback in the event external economic flows are ever scrutinized, contracts concurrently satisfy the statutory requirements for temporary-use leases (Act on Land and Building Leases Sec. 40) and fixed-term building leases with prior written disclosure (Sec. 38).
  3. **Exclusive Smart Lock API Sovereignty:** Smart lock access-control APIs for physical entryways are owned exclusively by Entity A. Upon contract expiration or loan termination due to trust breach (Civil Code Sec. 597/598), access revocation is executed lawfully as an exercise of Entity A's proprietary asset management rights.

* **United States Mode (US Mode) Compliance Circuit:**
  1. **Program Occupancy Agreement:** Utilizes non-residential corporate housing / extended-stay program exemptions excluded from state Residential Landlord-Tenant Acts, permitting Sheriff-led vacating within days upon agreement termination.
  2. **1099-NEC / AB5 ABC Test Full Compliance:** Enforces zero mandatory hours, zero behavioral control, and unrestricted multi-platform freedom via a purely asynchronous quest pool, satisfying Independent Contractor prongs (A, B, and C).
