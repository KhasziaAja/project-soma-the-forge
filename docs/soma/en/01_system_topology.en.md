## Overview & Purpose

This module defines the overarching system topology of **Project SOMA**, an autonomous social infrastructure that redefines human biological existence itself as an economic mining target via PAIOS (Physical-AI OS), driving individual net Living Maintenance Cost ($\text{Net\_LMC}$) to zero through in-kind R2S rebates. It establishes the strict boundary between the Public Layer and the Core Secret Layer (`SomaTopologyConfig`), the game-theoretic equilibrium against open-source fork/disintermediation attacks, and the Social Impact Bond (SIB) valuation model.

## 1.1 Structural Failure of Legacy Infrastructure & "Value in Existence"

The labor market and social security infrastructure in contemporary hyper-capitalist societies face a fatal dual structural misalignment.

First, industrial sectors (particularly Enterprise IT, finance, and manufacturing) irreversibly accumulate "stagnant tasks" (technical debt and data graveyards)—operations that are critical yet lack immediate urgency, thus overburdening the capacity of full-time employees, such as legacy code refactoring, system documentation, and unstructured data cleansing. Because these tasks demand high-level domain expertise, they cannot be resolved through conventional low-skilled dispatch labor, heavily crippling enterprise productivity. Meanwhile, highly skilled IT and specialist professionals unable to adapt to synchronous, co-located schedules and excessive interpersonal friction become socially isolated and underutilized.

Second, with the rapid advancement of Physical AI (humanoids, autonomous industrial machinery, and physical augmentation devices), the primary battleground of AI development has shifted from text and images to procuring millisecond-synchronized real-world human kinematics and environmental interaction data. Conventional simulation environments (Sim2Real) and laboratory subject recruitment fail to scale due to soaring labor costs, physical entropy limits (wear, stochastic variance, uneven terrain), and injury liability risks.

Project SOMA redefines human existence (Being) itself as a core system-driven module. Completely eliminating fragile psychological foundations such as expectations of morality or benevolence, the platform leverages fundamental human self-interest and personal payoff optimization as the operational engine to drive physical infrastructure (power, communications, logistics, labor). It shatters existing forced-labor models that commodify human time, directly monetizing physical existence—heart rate, gaze, electromyographic potential, ground reaction forces, and cognitive logs—as a continuous mining target via PAIOS (Physical-AI OS). This mathematical framework systematically drives individual net Living Maintenance Cost ($\text{Net\_LMC}$) down to zero via in-kind R2S offsets, achieving true de-commodification of time and establishing an inclusive social infrastructure.

* **PAIOS (Physical-AI OS):** An edge infrastructure that continuously captures and structures human physical motions and physiological signals (joint angles, torque, ground reaction force, heart rate variability, gaze) in real time, refining and supplying them as foundational model training data (e.g., Vision-Language-Action models) for Physical AI.

Public/Private Boundary Defense Game against open-source fork and disintermediation attacks by third-party operators or Big Tech competitors.

* **Actor 2 (SOMA Platform):** Action set $A_2 \in \{\text{Provide (Grant Black-Box API & Cleansing)}, \text{Block (Trigger Kill-Switch & Halt Refinement)}\}$

| | SOMA: Provide | SOMA: Block |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Actor 1: Cooperate** | $(R_{\text{sub}}, R_{\text{fee}})$ | $(0, 0)$ |

Thus, $(\text{Cooperate}, \text{Provide})$ holds as the unique Subgame Perfect Nash Equilibrium whenever $R_{\text{sub}} > 0$.

### 3) Mathematical Formulae

#### Formula 1.1: Net Living Maintenance Cost ($\text{Net\_LMC}$) In-Kind Offset Convergence Equation

To remain consistent with thermodynamic conservation (physical costs of shelter, energy, and nutrition never vanish), the system models the user's **out-of-pocket net burden** $\text{Net\_LMC}(t)$ converging to zero as R2S in-kind rebates offset baseline living costs:

$$\text{LMC}_{\text{gross}}(t) = \sum_{k=1}^{5} C_{\text{basic}, k}(t) > 0$$

$$\text{Net\_LMC}(t) = \max\left( 0,\; \text{LMC}_{\text{gross}}(t) - \sum_{m} R_{\text{rebate}, m}(t) \right)$$

$$\lim_{t \to \infty} \text{Net\_LMC}(t) = 0 \quad \left( \text{when } \sum_{m} R_{\text{rebate}, m}(t) \ge \text{LMC}_{\text{gross}}(t) \right)$$

* $k \in \{1..5\}$: The 5 essential living infrastructures (1: Housing, 2: Electricity & Water, 3: Communications, 4: Functional Nutrition, 5: Telemedicine & Insurance).
* $C_{\text{basic}, k}(t) > 0$: Wholesale procurement and supply cost of infrastructure item $k$.
* $R_{\text{rebate}, m}(t) \ge 0$: In-kind offset credits allocated from physical mining and guild rewards via the R2S pipeline.

#### Formula 1.2: SIB (Social Impact Bond) Municipal Cost Reduction Valuation ($V_{\text{SIB}}$)

$$V_{\text{SIB}}(t) = \sum_{i=1}^{N_{\text{resident}}(t)} \left( \Delta E_{\text{welfare}, i}(t) + \Delta E_{\text{medical}, i}(t) + \Delta E_{\text{unemp}, i}(t) \right)$$

$$P_{\text{SIB}}(t) = \rho_{\text{SIB}} \cdot \max\left( 0, \; V_{\text{SIB}}(t) - B_{\text{baseline}}(t) \right)$$

* $N_{\text{resident}}(t) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$: Number of active Project SOMA participants within the target municipality.
* $\Delta E_{\text{welfare}, i}, \Delta E_{\text{medical}, i}, \Delta E_{\text{unemp}, i}$: Verified reductions in public welfare payouts, subsidized medical expenses, and unemployment relief for participant $i$.
* $B_{\text{baseline}}(t) \ge 0$: Natural improvement baseline of the statistical control group (non-participating cohort).
* $\rho_{\text{SIB}} \in (0, 1)$: SIB performance-linked sharing rate (standard value $\rho_{\text{SIB}} = 0.50$).

### 4) Anti-Fragile Red-Teaming

1. **Attack Vector 1-A: Open-Source Code Reverse Engineering & Fork Attacks**
   * **Attack Scenario:** Competitor Big Tech or hardware manufacturers fork open-sourced PAIOS smart contracts and protocols to launch a parallel service, attempting to disintermediate the SOMA platform.
   * **Defensive Countermeasures:** Cloud-side AI cleansing weights that decode raw time-series kinematics into VLA-compatible robot control code are kept strictly behind proprietary APIs. Without passing through these black-box endpoints, raw data cannot be transformed into commercial-grade AI training datasets. Any unauthorized node attempting spoofed integration triggers an automated smart contract kill-switch, blocking access to data processing APIs and incentive pools.

2. **Attack Vector 1-B: Reputation Attacks & Dystopian Accusations (Narrative Poisoning)**
   * **Attack Scenario:** Media outlets or advocacy groups launch smear campaigns alleging that the platform exploits impoverished populations for biometric harvesting.
   * **Defensive Countermeasures:** All data processing applies zk-SNARKs by default, generating mathematical cryptographic proofs that privacy is absolute, which can be instantly presented to courts and media. Municipal partnerships follow standardized SIB frameworks audited by independent third parties, automatically publishing quantitative improvements in mental health, self-sufficiency, and savings generation.
